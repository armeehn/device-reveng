# VAD test fixtures

Raw 16 kHz mono PCM16 little-endian, used only by `SpeechGateTest`.

- `speech_01.s16`, `speech_03.s16`, `speech_10.s16`: two seconds each from LibriVox
  "Short Story Collection Vol. 109" (sections 01, 03, 10). LibriVox recordings are in the
  public domain.
- `noise_forest.s16`: five seconds of "Car Interior Ambience: Driving up a Forest Service
  Road" (archive.org, CC0).
- `noise_truck.s16`: five seconds of "Truck, Driving, Speeding" (interior, Designer's Choice
  Collection, archive.org, CC0).
