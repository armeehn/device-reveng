Field wiring guide · body CAN + LIN

Tap the RAV4 body signals → USB

Two buses, two taps. Door / lock / light / HVAC-request state rides the body CAN at the Smart-Key ECU. Full climate-panel detail — fan, mode, both temps — rides a separate LIN line at the A/C amplifier. Each needs its own adapter. All listen-only, no cut wires.

CAN 500 kbps · CANable LIN 19200 · transceiver → UART Car 2019 RAV4 Hybrid · XA50
Both taps are read-only On CAN, open slcan in listen-only mode (L\r) and disable the CANable's onboard terminator — the bus already has its two. On LIN, passively listen only; never let the transceiver drive the single-wire bus. Wire everything with the ignition off.

Tap 1 · body CAN — Smart-Key ECU

The Smart-Key / Certification ECU behind the passenger glove box carries the body CAN pair. Toyota withholds the exact pin numbers, so confirm CAN-H / CAN-L from the RAV4 EWD and a meter — don't trust wire color.

LineRoleTo CANableDB9 (CiA-303)
CAN-Hbus high, ~2.5 V idleCANHpin 7
CAN-Lbus low, ~2.5 V idleCANLpin 2
GNDoptional signal refGNDpin 3

  1. Drop the glove box & find the CAN pair

    Squeeze the sides past the stops or remove the liner. Identify CAN-H / CAN-L from the 2019 EWD — verify, don't assume.

  2. Confirm the bus with a meter

    Ignition off: ≈60 Ω across H↔L. Ignition on: ≈2.5 V each line to ground.

  3. Terminator off, then T-tap

    Set the CANable's 120 Ω jumper to off, then quick-splice CAN-H → CANH, CAN-L → CANL. No cutting.

  4. Bench it, then confirm IDs

    Laptop first — slcan init at 500 kbps (C\r · S6\r · L\r), run canable-probe.sh, watch for:

    0x620 doors / seatbelt 0x638 locks 0x614 blinkers 0x622 lights 0x590 HVAC request

Tap 2 · LIN — A/C amplifier (HVAC)

The body CAN only carries a coarse HVAC request (0x590). The full climate-panel state — fan speed, mode, both zone temps, A/C — lives on a LIN bus between the A/C amplifier (master) and the climate panel (slave). This is the well-decoded one (bitzero / ufnalski), and it's a different tap point and different hardware.

LIN ≠ CAN — you need a second adapter LIN is a single-wire bus, so the CANable can't read it. Tap the one LIN wire into a TJA1020 (or MCP2003) LIN transceiver feeding a USB-UART, and read it as plain serial at the bus baud. A ground reference to the transceiver is required here (unlike the CAN tap).
WhereBaudContent
A/C amplifier ↔ panel19200full climate state — decoded, tap here
Smart-Key ECU LIN9600present but undocumented — skip

Frame format: sync 0x55 + protected ID + 8 data bytes + checksum (inverted sum). The two frames that matter:

FrameDirByte map
0xB1 amp → panel b1 = fan 0–7 · b2 = mode (1 face · 2 face/feet · 3 feet · 4 feet/defrost · 9 defrost) · b4 = driver temp · b5 = passenger temp · b7 = A/C + illum bits
0x39 amp polls panel button presses — off / auto (b0), A/C / eco / fan± (b1), mode (b2), sync / defrost (b3), temp ± (b4/b5), recirc (b6)

  1. Reach the A/C amplifier

    It sits behind the center-stack / climate-panel assembly. Pull the panel trim to reach the amplifier connector carrying the LIN line to the HMI.

  2. Tap the single LIN wire + ground

    Backprobe the LIN signal into the TJA1020's LIN pin, and tie the transceiver ground to chassis. One signal wire — there's no pair.

  3. Read as serial at 19200

    Open the USB-UART at 19200 8N1 and log bytes. Frame on the 0x55 sync; slice out 0xB1 (status) and 0x39 (buttons).

  4. Toggle-and-diff to confirm

    Change fan / temp / mode on the physical panel and watch the matching byte move. That validates the byte map on your car before wiring it to the head unit.

Before you wire anything live Ignition off while splicing. Read-only on both buses — never inject on CAN, never drive the LIN line. Keep the CAN pinout discreet: those frames gate central locking.
